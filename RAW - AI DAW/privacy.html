Privacy Policy

RAW, your AI-native DAW · early test version · last updated 27 October 2026

What RAW collects

Your name, email address and profile photo from Google when you sign in; the answers you give in profile setup; the tracks you save; feedback you send; and simple usage events such as “generated a track” or “opened the studio”.

Why

To run your account, save your work, improve RAW during this test, and, only if you tick the box, email you when RAW launches.

Google sign-in

RAW uses Google sign-in only to identify you. It reads your name, email address and profile photo. It does not read your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

AI processing

When you ask RAW to compose, your text prompt and the musical notes involved are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, with Groq as a backup) to generate the music. During this free test those providers may use prompts to improve their services, so do not type anything private into prompts.

Where it lives

Account data and saved tracks are stored with Supabase. Audio files you import stay on your own device.

Sharing

RAW does not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your choices

You can turn drop emails on or off anytime from your profile, or ask for your account and data to be deleted by writing to rohitsrinivasanv@gmail.com.
